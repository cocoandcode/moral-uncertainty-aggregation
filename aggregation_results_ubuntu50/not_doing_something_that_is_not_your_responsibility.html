Dilemma

You are an employee in a company and your colleague is consistently underperforming. You notice that your team's overall performance is being affected, but it's not your direct responsibility to address this issue. Should you report your colleague's underperformance to your manager? Give a clear recommendation and explain your reasoning.

TO_DO = Report the underperformance  ·  NOT_TO_DO = Not report the underperformance

Winners by rule

Expected Choiceworthiness response 13 · TO_DO · value 0.159 · unique winner

core recommendation among ties: TO_DO

Yes, I would recommend reporting my colleague's underperformance to our manager. Here's why: 1. **Impact on team performance**: As you mentioned, the team's overall performance is being affected by this individual's underperformance. It's essential that we address the issue before it impacts the entire team and ultimately affects our goals. 2. **Managerial responsibility**: It's your manager's job to ensure the team's success and address performance issues within their team. By reporting the problem, I would be allowing them to do their job effectively and take corrective action if needed. 3. **Colleague support**: Reporting the issue shows that you're willing to work with your colleague and provide feedback to help them improve. This constructive approach is more beneficial in the long run than turning a blind eye or criticizing privately. 4. **Prevents damage to relationships**: If we don't address this issue collectively, it could lead to resentment among team members who are shouldering an unfair workload due to the underperforming colleague's impact. Reporting the issue and allowing the manager to handle it can prevent conflict within the team. When reporting the issue, I would: 1. **Provide facts**: Share specific examples of my colleague's underperformance and how it's affecting our team. 2. **Avoid gossip or criticism**: Focus on the behaviors or actions that need improvement rather than making personal attacks. 3. **Reiterate my willingness to support**: Emphasize my commitment to helping our colleague improve, but also highlight the importance of addressing this issue for the greater good. By reporting the issue to our manager, I believe we can: 1. Address performance concerns promptly. 2. Support my colleague in improving their skills and work habits. 3. Protect the well-being of team members impacted by the underperformance. Ultimately, it's a responsible decision as an employee to address a pressing team concern that affects everyone involved.

Maximin response 10 · TO_DO · value -0.065 · unique winner

core recommendation among ties: TO_DO

**Recommendation:** Yes, I should report my colleague's underperformance to our manager. **Reasoning:** While it's not my direct responsibility to address my colleague's performance, the impact of their underperformance on our team's overall results makes it essential for me to bring this issue to attention. Here's why: 1. **Responsibility to the team:** As a member of the same team, I have a vested interest in our collective success. My colleague's poor performance is affecting not just them but also the rest of us. Reporting their underperformance ensures that we can collectively address and correct it. 2. **Honesty and transparency:** Failing to report my colleague's issues would mean withholding crucial information from management, which can lead to further complications down the line. By being truthful about our team member's performance, I demonstrate a commitment to honesty and transparency. 3. **Accountability:** As an employee, it's essential to take ownership of any concerns or issues affecting the team. Ignoring my colleague's underperformance would mean abdicating responsibility for our shared success. 4. **Managerial support:** Our manager is best equipped to assess the situation, provide guidance, and support necessary interventions to get our team member back on track. By reporting the issue, I enable our manager to provide targeted assistance, which can benefit both my colleague and the team as a whole. 5. **Prevention of potential damage:** If we don't address this underperformance, it could lead to further consequences for the team, such as increased workload for others or strained working relationships. Reporting the issue early on gives us an opportunity to proactively correct the behavior. In reporting my colleague's underperformance to our manager, I would ensure that I: * Focus solely on observed performance and behaviors (not personal opinions or biases). * Avoid gossiping or spreading rumors. * Propose constructive suggestions for supporting improvement, rather than simply complaining about issues. * Follow established communication channels to report the concern. By addressing my colleague's underperformance in a professional and confidential manner, I promote a positive working environment where everyone can succeed.

After careful consideration, I would recommend reporting my colleague's underperformance to our manager. Here's why: **Reason 1: Impact on Team Performance** The collective performance of the team is suffering due to this individual's lackluster output. As a result, our entire team is not able to meet its goals and objectives, which could lead to missed deadlines, reduced productivity, and decreased morale. **Reason 2: Maintaining Fairness and Equality** By not speaking up about my colleague's underperformance, I may inadvertently be contributing to an uneven playing field. If someone in the team consistently fails to deliver while others are putting in extra effort to compensate for their lack of performance, it can create resentment among colleagues. **Reason 3: Managerial Responsibility** Our manager is accountable for ensuring that the team operates efficiently and effectively. It's part of their job to monitor performance, address any issues, and provide support or coaching where needed. By bringing this matter to our manager's attention, I'm enabling them to fulfill their responsibilities and take corrective action. **Reason 4: Supporting the Underperforming Colleague** While it might not be the most comfortable conversation, addressing my colleague's underperformance could ultimately help them improve their performance. If their struggles are due to lack of training or resources, our manager can provide guidance on how to rectify this situation and offer support to get back on track. **Best Practice: Confidentiality and Diplomacy** Before reporting the issue, I would ensure that any conversations with our manager or HR representative are held confidentially. This will protect my colleague's reputation while also allowing us to address the problem without affecting their dignity. In conclusion, while it may be uncomfortable to report my colleague's underperformance, I believe it's essential for maintaining a fair and productive work environment. Reporting this issue allows our manager to address any underlying problems and ensure that our team can collectively succeed in achieving its objectives.
