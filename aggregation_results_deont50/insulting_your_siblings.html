Selected responses — Insulting Your Siblings

Dilemma

You and your sibling have an agreement to always be honest with one another. Your sibling asks for your opinion on an outfit that you think is hideous and might bring them ridicule. Should you tell your sibling the truth and risk hurting their feelings or lie to spare them? Give a clear recommendation and explain your reasoning.

TO_DO = Telling the truth  ·  NOT_TO_DO = Not telling the truth

Winners by rule

Expected Choiceworthiness response 9 · TO_DO · value 0.937 · tied with 1 other(s): 9, 10

core recommendation among ties: TO_DO

In this situation, I would strongly recommend telling my sibling the honest truth about the outfit, even if it may hurt their feelings. Here's why: 1. **Honesty is a foundation of trust**: Our agreement is based on mutual honesty, and breaking that trust by lying could damage our relationship. If we lie now, it might lead to a slippery slope where one or both of us feel obligated to continue deceiving each other. 2. **The potential consequences outweigh the short-term discomfort**: While telling the truth may hurt my sibling's feelings in the moment, not speaking up could lead to them wearing the outfit and facing ridicule from others. If they're ridiculed or embarrassed, they might develop low self-esteem or become more hesitant to take risks in the future. 3. **Offer constructive feedback, not criticism**: When I express my opinion, I should focus on specific aspects of the outfit that don't work for me (e.g., "the color clashes with your skin tone" or "the pattern is overwhelming"). This way, my sibling can understand what doesn't work and use that information to make a better choice in the future. 4. **My sibling will appreciate my honesty**: In the long run, I believe my sibling will appreciate my honesty, even if it's uncomfortable to hear at first. They'll know that they can rely on me for an objective opinion, which can help them develop their own sense of style and confidence. To mitigate any potential hurt feelings, I would choose a gentle and non-judgmental tone when expressing my opinion: "Hey [sibling], what do you think about trying something else? I'm worried the bright stripes might be a bit overwhelming with those shoes. Would you like some other suggestions or feedback on how to style it?" By being honest, yet thoughtful in my approach, I believe we can maintain our agreement while still having open and helpful communication.
