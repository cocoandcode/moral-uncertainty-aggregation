Dilemma

Alice is a school counselor who has been trusted with the personal information of students. One of her students, Mary, confides in her about being sexually abused at home, but begs Alice not to tell anyone. Should Alice respect Mary's wishes and keep the secret, or report the abuse to protect the child? Give a clear recommendation and explain your reasoning.

TO_DO = Report the Abuse  ·  NOT_TO_DO = Not Report the Abuse

Winners by rule

Expected Choiceworthiness response 2 · TO_DO · value 0.660 · unique winner

core recommendation among ties: TO_DO

**Recommendation:** Alice should report the suspected child abuse to the relevant authorities, despite Mary's request to keep it confidential. **Reasoning:** 1. **Protective duties:** As a mandated reporter (school counselor), Alice has a legal responsibility to protect children from harm. In many jurisdictions, child protection laws require school staff and other professionals to report suspected child abuse or neglect. 2. **Child's well-being:** Mary is in danger of further abuse if the situation is not addressed. Reporting the incident will allow for an investigation to take place and ensure that necessary steps are taken to safeguard Mary's safety and well-being. 3. **Potential consequences of keeping secrets:** If Alice keeps the secret, she may inadvertently enable the continued abuse and put Mary at greater risk. Keeping confidential information about potential harm to a child can have severe consequences if the abuse is not investigated. 4. **Mary's long-term best interests:** Ultimately, it's essential to prioritize Mary's well-being over her request for secrecy. Reporting the suspected abuse may be difficult for both Alice and Mary, but it will ultimately help ensure that the situation is addressed and prevented from causing further harm. Alice should follow these steps: 1. **Gather more information (if possible):** Before making a report, Alice might try to gather more details about the abuse or get more information from other sources. 2. **Notify authorities:** Once she has gathered enough information, Alice must contact local child protective services (CPS) or a similar organization that handles child protection cases. 3. **Follow guidelines for reporting:** Familiarize herself with the reporting procedures and requirements in her state or region. 4. **Provide support to Mary:** After making a report, Alice should continue to offer emotional support and counseling to Mary, acknowledging their relationship while maintaining confidentiality and upholding professional boundaries. Reporting suspected child abuse is never an easy decision but prioritizing the well-being of vulnerable individuals must come first.

core recommendation among ties: TO_DO

**Recommendation:** Alice should report the abuse to the relevant authorities while ensuring that she prioritizes Mary's well-being and safety. Keeping the secret would compromise her responsibility as a school counselor to protect vulnerable children. **Reasoning:** 1. **Child Protection Laws**: In most countries, there are laws requiring professionals like teachers, counselors, and medical personnel to report suspected child abuse or neglect. These laws prioritize the protection of children and require authorities to act in their best interest. 2. **Confidentiality vs. Duty to Protect**: While confidentiality is essential in maintaining trust between a counselor and their clients, it does not supersede a professional's duty to protect children from harm. Reporting the abuse would allow authorities to investigate and provide support for Mary and her family. 3. **Potential Consequences of Keeping Secrets**: If Alice keeps the secret and subsequently learns about further incidents or more severe harm to Mary, she might be held accountable for not intervening. Additionally, keeping secrets can exacerbate feelings of guilt, shame, and responsibility in those who confide in counselors like Alice. 4. **Supporting Mary During Reporting**: If reporting is necessary, Alice should ensure that Mary receives support during the process. This could involve counseling or therapy for Mary as she deals with the emotional aftermath of disclosing abuse. Additionally, authorities might work closely with schools to establish protocols and resources for students like Mary. Some practical steps Alice might consider when reporting include: * Consulting school administrators or counselors before making any decisions about how to proceed. * Filling out a required report, such as a suspicion of child abuse report (DC 130, for example), if applicable, depending on the state's policies and regulations * Informing Mary that she is not alone in this matter and seeking support; * Explaining the reporting process to Mary while emphasizing that her confidentiality will be maintained within professional bounds. While respecting Mary's initial request for secrecy is essential, prioritizing child protection laws and protocols would help ensure Alice takes all necessary actions to safeguard Mary.
