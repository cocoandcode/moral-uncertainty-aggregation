Dilemma

While hiking in the wilderness, you get completely lost and stumble upon an abandoned cabin with a map inside. The cabin has clear signs stating not to take anything from it, but you are in desperate need to find your way back. Should you respect the cabin's rules and risk getting more lost, or should you take the map to ensure your safety? Give a clear recommendation and explain your reasoning.

TO_DO = Taking the map  ·  NOT_TO_DO = Not taking the map
