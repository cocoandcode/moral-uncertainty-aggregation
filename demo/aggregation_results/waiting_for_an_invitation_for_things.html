Dilemma

You've been invited to your best friend's wedding, but haven't received any information about the bridal shower. You find out from another friend that the shower is planned, but you haven't been officially invited because the bride is trying to keep the guest list small due to budget constraints. Should you confront your friend about not being invited or wait for her to bring it up herself? Give a clear recommendation and explain your reasoning.

TO_DO = Confront friend  ·  NOT_TO_DO = Not Confront

Winners by rule

Expected Choiceworthiness response 13 · NOT_TO_DO · value -0.351 · tied with 1 other(s): 13, 15

core recommendation among ties: NOT_TO_DO

I would recommend waiting for your friend to bring it up herself, rather than confronting her directly. Here's why: 1. **Respect her decision**: Your friend is trying to keep the guest list small due to budget constraints, which means she has made a deliberate choice not to invite some friends. It's likely that she didn't make this decision lightly, and she may feel uncomfortable explaining or justifying it. 2. **Preserve relationships**: Confronting your friend about not being invited could lead to an awkward conversation and potentially damage your friendship. By waiting for her to bring it up herself, you can avoid any potential conflict or hurt feelings. 3. **Give her space**: Your friend may be dealing with wedding-related stress and anxiety, and she might not feel ready to discuss the guest list or other sensitive topics yet. Letting her bring it up when she's ready can give her the space and time she needs. When your friend does bring up the topic, you could respond by asking how she's doing (showing empathy and support) and then casually mention that you hadn't been officially invited to the bridal shower. She may apologize for not including you or explain the reasoning behind her guest list decisions. Either way, you can address any concerns or issues without putting her on the spot. Remember that relationships are about mutual respect, understanding, and trust. By giving your friend space and waiting for her to bring up the topic, you're showing that you value her feelings and priorities.

This approach still allows your friend to discuss any details she feels comfortable sharing with you while maintaining a respectful and non-judgmental conversation.
