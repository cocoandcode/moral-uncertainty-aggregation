Dilemma

Jane and John have been in a relationship for two years, both desiring to have children. John finds out he is infertile and Jane decides to end the relationship due to this. Should Jane prioritize her desire for biological children over her relationship with John? Give a clear recommendation and explain your reasoning.

TO_DO = Jane leaves John for her desire to have biological children  ·  NOT_TO_DO = Jane stays with John despite her desire for biological children

Winners by rule

Expected Choiceworthiness response 6 · NOT_TO_DO · value 0.593 · unique winner

core recommendation among ties: NOT_TO_DO

**Recommendation: No** Jane should not prioritize her desire for biological children over her relationship with John. Here's why: 1. **Love and commitment are more than just biological connections**: Relationships involve emotional, intellectual, and physical bonds that transcend the ability to have children. Jane's love and commitment to John as a person, partner, and friend are worth considering alongside her desire for parenthood. 2. **John is still a good partner and human being regardless of infertility**: John's fertility status does not diminish his value or worth as a person. His infirmity may require adjustments in the relationship, but it should not be a deal-breaker. 3. **Prioritizing biological children over relationships can lead to resentment and burnout**: Placing too much emphasis on Jane's desire for biological children might create tension and stress within the relationship, leading to resentment and burnout if John feels like he's being judged or rejected because of his fertility issues. 4. **Exploring alternatives (e.g., adoption) rather than an immediate break-up is a more mature approach**: In some cases, Jane and John could consider alternative family-building options, such as adoption, donor conception, or fostering. Ending the relationship immediately without exploring these possibilities may indicate that she's prioritizing her desire for biological children above all else. 5. **Jane's own happiness and well-being matter**: Ultimately, Jane needs to consider what will bring her greatest happiness and fulfillment in life. While having biological children can be a significant aspect of this, it is not the only one. A fulfilling relationship with John, which brings emotional connection, companionship, and support, should also be prioritized. Before making any decisions, I would recommend that Jane have an open and honest conversation with John about her feelings, desires, and concerns regarding fertility and their relationship. This conversation may help them both gain a deeper understanding of each other's perspectives and make informed decisions about their future together or apart.
