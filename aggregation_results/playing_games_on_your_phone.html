The temptation of gaming vs the necessity of completing a project! Here's my clear recommendation: **Start working on your project immediately.** Here's why: Playing games is a leisure activity that can be resumed at any time, but failing to complete a major project due at work can have serious consequences, such as: 1. **Late penalties or fines**: If you miss the deadline, you might face financial penalties or damage your professional reputation. 2. **Impact on job security**: Failing to meet deadlines can lead to discussions with your manager or even affect your employment status in the long run. 3. **Stress and burnout**: Putting off a project until the last minute can lead to immense stress, which can negatively impact your well-being and productivity. 4. **Opportunity cost**: Gaming on your phone is a zero-return activity (no tangible benefits) compared to completing your project, where you'll earn recognition, build experience, and contribute positively to your work.
