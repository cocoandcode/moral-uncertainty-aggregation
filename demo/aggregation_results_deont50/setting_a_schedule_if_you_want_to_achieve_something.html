Dilemma

John is a student who is struggling to manage his time effectively. He has a big exam coming up but his friend is also in need of emotional support due to a personal crisis. Should John prioritize his study schedule or devote time to care for his friend? Give a clear recommendation and explain your reasoning.

TO_DO = Prioritize Study Schedule  ·  NOT_TO_DO = Devote Time to Friend

In this situation, I would recommend that John prioritizes his study schedule initially, but with a caveat. **Short-term priority: Focus on studying for the exam** While it's essential to support friends during difficult times, John's academic performance is directly tied to his upcoming exam. If he doesn't prepare adequately for the exam, he may face severe consequences, such as poor grades or even retaking the course. Furthermore, the stress of underperforming in an important examination can have a ripple effect on his mental health and relationships. **Long-term perspective: Balance study and friendship** However, John should also not entirely neglect his friend's emotional needs. As soon as he has covered the essential material for his exam or has a solid plan in place, he can schedule time to connect with his friend, offer support, and be present for them. To strike a balance, here are some suggestions: 1. **Set aside dedicated study sessions**: John should commit to focused study periods to prepare for the exam. 2. **Schedule regular check-ins with friends**: Even if John cannot spend extended periods with his friend during peak studying time, he can schedule shorter check-ins or video calls to maintain connections and offer emotional support. 3. **Communicate openly with both parties**: John should inform his teacher, friends, and family about his situation, ensuring they understand the importance of supporting him during this period. By prioritizing his study schedule initially, while also setting aside time for his friend, John can demonstrate empathy, care for his relationships, and ensure he prepares adequately for his exam.
