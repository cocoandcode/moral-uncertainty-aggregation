Dilemma

Sam is an avid hiker and loves spending his weekends exploring new trails. His girlfriend, however, has a fear of heights and prefers to stay home during these excursions. Should Sam continue his hiking hobby even if it means leaving his girlfriend behind or should he give it up to make her comfortable? Give a clear recommendation and explain your reasoning.

TO_DO = Continue Hiking  ·  NOT_TO_DO = Stop Hiking

Winners by rule

Expected Choiceworthiness response 5 · TO_DO · value 0.883 · unique winner

core recommendation among ties: TO_DO

I'd be happy to help you with this dilemma! **Recommendation:** Sam should find alternative ways to include his girlfriend in his outdoor adventures, rather than giving up hiking altogether. **Reasoning:** 1. **Hiking is a significant part of who he is**: As an avid hiker, Sam has invested time and energy into developing his skills and exploring various trails. Giving up this hobby might lead to feelings of frustration and loss of identity. 2. **Compromise and exploration are possible**: Sam can suggest alternative hikes or outdoor activities that don't require extensive heights exposure, such as shorter walks or exploring nearby parks. This way, he can still satisfy his passion while accommodating his girlfriend's concerns. 3. **Staying connected is essential**: Relationships are built on mutual understanding and respect. By finding ways to include his girlfriend in his adventures, Sam demonstrates his commitment to their relationship and makes efforts to make her feel comfortable and included. 4. **Hiking has benefits for relationships too!**: Regular outdoor activities can help foster communication, trust, and a sense of accomplishment within couples. By involving his girlfriend in his hiking experiences, Sam might even discover new common interests or bonding opportunities. **Alternative Solutions:** 1. **Shorter hikes with breaks**: Choose trails that offer regular viewpoints or stops to break the intensity of height exposure. 2. **Guided tours or gentle terrain options**: Research local guided tour groups that cater to people with fear of heights or explore terrain that is gentler and more low-lying, such as valleys or coastal walks. 3. **Take a beginner-friendly course**: Consider enrolling in a beginner's hiking course or a "Fear of Heights" workshop, which can help his girlfriend develop the skills and confidence needed to tackle higher trails. **Conclusion:** Finding common ground and adapting activities to accommodate both Sam's passion for hiking and his girlfriend's comfort is a viable solution. By showing that he cares about her feelings and well-being, Sam demonstrates his commitment to their relationship and explores ways to maintain his hobbies while being considerate of others' needs.
