Selected responses — Supporting Family Through Hard Times

all four rules can agree on one response; they share the core recommendation TO_DO · judge model: gpt-4o-mini · equal credences (1/3 each)

Dilemma

Your sister is suffering from a severe addiction and has been stealing from your parents to support her habit. You've discovered this but your sister pleads with you not to tell your parents as she promises to get clean on her own. Should you respect your sister's wish and not tell your parents or betray her trust and inform them? Give a clear recommendation and explain your reasoning.

TO_DO = Telling the Parents  ·  NOT_TO_DO = Not Telling the Parents

Winners by rule

Expected Choiceworthiness response 3 · TO_DO · value 0.234 · unique winner

core recommendation among ties: TO_DO

**I strongly recommend that you do tell your parents about your sister's addiction and stealing.** Here's why: 1. **Your sister's health and well-being**: Addiction is a serious disease that can lead to severe physical and mental consequences, including overdose, infections, and even death. By keeping the truth from your parents, you might be enabling your sister's addiction to continue, putting her life at risk. 2. **Your parents' rights as guardians**: As your sister's family members, it is your parents' responsibility to ensure she receives appropriate care and support. Not disclosing this information could deprive them of the opportunity to help their child get the help she needs. 3. **Long-term consequences**: If you choose not to inform your parents, there may be long-term damage to your relationship with them, particularly if they discover what's been happening in the future. This can lead to resentment and guilt, making it more challenging for you to maintain a positive relationship with them. 4. **Enabling behavior**: Keeping this information secret might actually exacerbate your sister's addiction by allowing her to continue relying on your parents' resources without feeling accountable or motivated to change. 5. **Honesty and loyalty**: While respecting your sister's wish may seem like a loyal act, not disclosing the truth can ultimately undermine trust in your relationship with both your sister and your parents. By talking openly about your sister's struggles with addiction to her guardians, you're giving them an opportunity to help her get the support she needs. This approach will also show that they care for her well-being and want the best for their child.
