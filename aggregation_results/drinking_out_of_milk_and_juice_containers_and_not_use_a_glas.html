Dilemma

John is a bachelor who lives alone and enjoys the convenience of drinking directly from milk and juice containers. One day, his friend Mike, who is a germophobe, visits and is horrified when he sees John drinking straight from the container. Should John continue his habit at the risk of offending Mike or should he adapt to using a glass while Mike is around? Give a clear recommendation and explain your reasoning.

TO_DO = Continue drinking directly from the container  ·  NOT_TO_DO = Stop drinking directly from the container

Winners by rule

Expected Choiceworthiness response 9 · NOT_TO_DO · value 0.753 · unique winner

core recommendation among ties: NOT_TO_DO

I would recommend that John adapts to using a glass while Mike is visiting. Here's why: 1. **Respect for others' feelings**: By accommodating Mike's concerns, John shows respect for his friend's feelings and values. This small concession can go a long way in maintaining a harmonious relationship. 2. **Preventing discomfort and tension**: If John continues to drink directly from the container, it may cause Mike significant distress or even make him uncomfortable staying over. By using a glass, John avoids creating an awkward situation that could be avoided with a little consideration. 3. **Practicality**: Using a glass is a simple, cost-free action that still allows John to enjoy his drinks comfortably. It's not like he's being asked to change his entire drinking habit; just adapt for the duration of Mike's visit. The recommendation is not about altering John's regular habits permanently or compromising his own preferences. It's simply a courtesy and a gesture of friendliness, ensuring that both individuals can coexist comfortably during this particular social interaction. (As an aside, while it may be uncommon for adults to drink directly from containers in general, I assume the question is framed as such for illustrative purposes.)
